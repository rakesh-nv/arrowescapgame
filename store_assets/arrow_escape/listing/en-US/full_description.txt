Tap an arrow and it slides off the board along its own path. Sounds easy, until the arrows start blocking each other.

Arrow Escape is a minimalist logic puzzle about order. Every board is a maze of bent, winding arrows. An arrow can only escape if the lane in front of its head is clear all the way to the edge. Find the arrows that are free, clear them, and open the way for the next ones. Tap a blocked arrow and you lose a life, so think before you tap.
